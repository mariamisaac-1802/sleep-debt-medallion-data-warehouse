Key Analytical Insights from the Medallion Data Warehouse

📱 Screen Time Inflection Point (60+ min)

Users spending ≥60 minutes on phones in bed experience an average sleep latency of 50.7 minutes and only 5.56 hours of sleep, with 78% reporting moderate or severe sleep debt.

🎬 Short-Form Video Disruptor

Users who engage with TikTok / Reels and Instagram / Reddit before bed suffer the lowest average sleep (5.88h) and highest next-day fatigue scores (4.16/10), outranking long-form streaming.

🦉 Night Owls in Corporate Schedules

Night owls in Corporate 9-to-5 positions endure the lowest sleep duration (5.41 hours) and highest morning alarm snoozes (2.41 times), highlighting social jetlag misalignment.

💡 Blue Light Filter Efficacy

Activating the blue light filter shortens sleep latency by an average of 4.2 minutes and preserves deep sleep percentage, offering a modest protective buffer against display light.
